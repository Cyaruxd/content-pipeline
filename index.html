Content Pipeline

Content Pipeline is a personal tool that its owner runs on their own computer to publish short educational videos to their own YouTube channel and review how those videos perform.

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos to the owner's YouTube channel, with titles, descriptions and scheduled publish times.
  • Reads view counts and audience-retention statistics for the owner's own videos, to decide what to make next.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data. It uses YouTube API Services.
